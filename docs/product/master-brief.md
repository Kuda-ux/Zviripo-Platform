# Comodities Master Product Brief

## Vision

Build a digital operating layer for Zimbabwe's everyday economy. It must help consumers find nearby products and opportunities; merchants run businesses online or offline; service providers find customers; job seekers find real work; and, in later phases, farmers find buyers.

Core promise: **Find it. Sell it. Need it. Build with it.**

## Product surfaces

- Consumer mobile/web: home, discover, products, shops, sell, requests, services, jobs, enquiries/messages, saves, receipts, profile, and trust.
- Merchant mobile: dashboard, POS, products, inventory, sales, customers, credit/on-book, QR receipts, marketplace publishing, insights, verification, and business profile.
- Admin web: operations overview, users, merchants, listings, moderation, verification, metrics, marketplace health, sync/device health, intelligence, and audit logs.

## MVP

Consumer discovery, marketplace listings, search/filter/location, merchant profiles and onboarding, offline POS, inventory, sales, customer credit, QR receipts, linked POS/marketplace stock, basic messaging/enquiry, basic trust/verification, admin moderation and operations, analytics, and sync monitoring.

## Delivery order

1. Repository conventions
2. Design tokens and primitives
3. Supabase schema, migrations, and RLS
4. Authentication and roles
5. Mobile shell/navigation
6. Consumer marketplace
7. Merchant onboarding
8. Merchant SQLite/POS
9. Offline sync engine
10. Marketplace/POS integration
11. Admin foundation
12. Trust and safety
13. Intelligence
14. AI enhancements

## Product constraints

Zimbabwe-first connectivity and hardware constraints are requirements. Make complexity disappear. Offline language should reassure merchants that business can continue. Notifications must be sparse and useful. Search and local relevance dominate home. Trust is a feature. Privacy precedes analytics. AI follows reliable data. Regulated payment integrations may be added later, but the platform must not become an unlicensed payment processor.

## Future phases

Services/jobs expansion, business reliability history, aggregated Price Radar and Demand Radar, group procurement, agriculture-specific workflows, grounded AI assistance, and regulated payment integrations. These must not compromise MVP focus.
